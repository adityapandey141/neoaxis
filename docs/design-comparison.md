# Design Comparison — Concept A vs Concept B

## Overview

Both concepts share the same verified content, data layer, and reusable components (including a shared image carousel of 6 original NeoAxis event photographs).
They differ fundamentally in layout system, typography, colour balance, hero composition, section rhythm, and editorial voice.

## Concept A — "NeoAxis Precision"
**Route:** `/demo/neoaxis-precision`

### Design Philosophy
Premium, contemporary pharmaceutical corporate site. Conveys trust, credibility, and scale.

### Key Visual Characteristics
- **Layout:** Clean grid; symmetrical, card-based sections; generous whitespace
- **Typography:** Inter throughout (geometric neo-grotesque sans-serif); clean and uniform
- **Colour:** White and soft-neutral surfaces; deep navy primary; orange as restrained accent
- **Hero:** Full-bleed photographic hero with gradient overlay; positioning headline "Better Access to Essential Medicine. Stronger Communities."; two prominent CTAs
- **Header:** Sticky, transparent-to-solid on scroll with rounded CTA button
- **Impact band:** Three-column stat strip with highlighted "5 lakh+" figure, community health camps, and growing geographical presence
- **New: How We Support Better Health:** Three-card section on access, literacy, and community-focused solutions
- **Products:** 3-column grid of expandable disclosure cards with improved spacing
- **New: Healthcare in Action:** Side-by-side layout — descriptive text on left, 6-image carousel on right, corporate container
- **Full-width community photo:** Gradient overlay with message, more generous height
- **Pillars:** 3×2 grid with numbered (01–06) treatments and subtle hover states
- **Publications:** 4-column grid, clickable with shadow hover effects
- **Leadership:** Rounded-rectangle portraits (larger), centred layout with improved spacing
- **Contact CTA:** Photo-backed band with navy overlay
- **Footer:** Three-column structured footer with navigation, contact info, and tagline

### Strengths
- Familiar corporate pharma layout, immediately credible
- Rich imagery with the 6-image carousel providing real evidence of fieldwork
- New "How We Support" section adds clarity to the company's approach
- Structured and scannable at all screen sizes

## Concept B — "Medicine with a Cause"
**Route:** `/demo/medicine-with-a-cause`

### Design Philosophy
Human-centred, editorial design. Tells a story; feels personal and purposeful.

### Key Visual Characteristics
- **Layout:** Asymmetric columns; offset grids; deliberately uneven spacing
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (expressive serif) for headlines and pull-quotes; Inter for body
- **Colour:** Warm ivory background; navy text; orange used sparingly for numbers and accents
- **Hero:** Split-screen editorial: text on left with large serif "Medicine with a Cause", tall photo on right (7-column span), overlapping stat card
- **Header:** Airy feel; rounded-full pill CTA
- **Company story:** Asymmetric two-image grid (7:5 ratio with offset) alongside editorial text
- **New: Purpose in Practice:** Three editorial numbered columns (01–03) covering access, literacy, and community empowerment
- **Vision:** Full-width photo overlay with decorative quotation mark SVG, reduced overlay opacity for better photo visibility
- **New: Real Moments, Shared Purpose:** Sticky sidebar text with 6-image editorial carousel, storytelling-led presentation
- **Products:** Horizontal chapter layout with numbered categories (01–05), more generous vertical spacing
- **Pillars:** Side-by-side layout with 12-column grid, large numbered editorial list with wider number column
- **Publications:** Offset 3-column grid with shadow hover, clickable items
- **Leadership:** Rectangular portraits, full aspect ratio, editorial caption treatment
- **Contact CTA:** Photo-backed band with rounded-full buttons
- **Footer:** Three-column structured footer matching editorial tone

### Strengths
- Distinctive editorial voice; stands out from typical pharma sites
- Strong storytelling through the "Real Moments" carousel section
- "Purpose in Practice" adds thematic structure without repeating the intro
- More emotional connection through typography and layout variety

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Dimension | Concept A (Precision) | Concept B (Cause) |
|---|---|---|
| Overall feel | Corporate, structured, trustworthy | Editorial, warm, storytelling |
| Primary font | Inter (sans-serif only) | Playfair Display + Inter |
| Background | White / light gray alternating | Ivory canvas with white sections |
| Hero headline | "Better Access to Essential Medicine. Stronger Communities." | "Medicine with a Cause" (serif, two-line) |
| Hero photo | Full-bleed with side gradient | Tall portrait crop with stat overlay card |
| Impact data | Three-column stat strip | Stat card in hero + numbered impact sidebar |
| New section 1 | "How We Support Better Health" (3 icon cards) | "Purpose in Practice" (3 numbered editorial columns) |
| New section 2 | "Healthcare in Action" (side-by-side carousel) | "Real Moments, Shared Purpose" (sticky sidebar + carousel) |
| Carousel style | Clean container, square controls, pill dots | Rounded container, circular controls, rounded dots |
| Products | Expandable card grid | Numbered chapter layout |
| Pillars | 3×2 numbered grid cards | Side-by-side numbered editorial list |
| Publications | 4-column even grid | 3-column offset grid |
| Leadership | Rounded-rectangle crops, centered | Full rectangular crops, left-aligned editorial |
| Button style | Rounded rectangle (rounded-lg) | Pill (rounded-full) |
| CTA colour | Orange fill primary | Navy fill primary |
| Footer | 3-column with structured sections | 3-column with editorial tone |

## Recommendation
Present both to client leadership. Concept A is safer for a pharma audience accustomed to
structured corporate sites. Concept B differentiates NeoAxis from competitors and better
reflects "Medicine with a Cause" as a mission-driven brand. Both now include real company
photography extensively, and the shared carousel component ensures the original event
photographs are prominently featured in both concepts.
